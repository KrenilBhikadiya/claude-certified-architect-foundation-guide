## The exam in brief

60 items, multiple-choice and multiple-response · 4 scenarios drawn at random from 6 · 120 minutes · pass at a scaled 720 / 1,000.

## How accurate is it?

The study guide was fact-checked on 2026-10-30 against complete copies of 65 official doc pages (about 1,040 claims checked, about 120 corrections; evidence in [`study_guide/verification/`](study_guide/verification/)). Some pages were read by section rather than end to end, a few details are marked "(verify in docs)", and patterns that exist only in the exam guide have no doc page to check against. The README in `study_guide/` lists these limits.
